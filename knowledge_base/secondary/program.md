## Why Study AI (syllabus p.7)
→
→In 2026, organizations across industries will continue shifting 
from experimentation to real AI adoption, creating a 
strong need for professionals who can connect business 
goals with technical execution.
Companies don’t just need engineers they need AI 
consultants who can translate between teams, define 
ethical use cases, and deliver projects that are 
efficient, compliant, and measurable.
The growing impact of EU AI regulations makes the ability to 
evaluate bias, fairness, and sustainability a must-have skill 
for any organization deploying AI systems.
As AI tools become more integrated into everyday 
workflows, businesses are prioritizing human-AI 
collaboration, automation, and responsible innovation all 
areas where trained AI consultants add immediate value.
This bootcamp prepares you to lead and deliver AI-
driven transformations, combining technical 
fluency, ethical frameworks, and business strategy 
to ensure successful adoption and measurable 
outcomes.

## What this Course is About  (syllabus p.12)
Helping organizations unlock the power of AI
This course prepares you to guide companies through the process 
of adopting AI, from identifying opportunities to implementing real 
solutions that deliver measurable impact.
Mastering the AI consulting process
You’ll learn how to assess business needs, evaluate data readiness, 
and design AI-driven strategies that align with ethical, 
operational, and financial goals.
Bridging business and technology
You’ll gain a deep understanding of how AI models, APIs, and 
automation tools integrate into existing systems learning to 
translate between technical teams and business leaders.
Responsible and structured implementation
Through real-world case studies you’ll practice building 
roadmaps, managing stakeholders, and applying frameworks like 
the EU AI Act to ensure compliance and responsible adoption.
Driving change with confidence
By the end, you’ll be able to lead AI transformation projects guiding 
teams from strategy to execution while ensuring solutions are 
ethical, efficient, and aligned with organizational goals.
